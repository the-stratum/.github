# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

STRATUM is an independent engineering and creative collective built around thoughtful collaboration, technical excellence, creativity, and mutual respect.

We are committed to maintaining an environment where people can participate constructively, exchange ideas, share knowledge, and contribute without harassment or discrimination.

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone participating in STRATUM projects and community spaces, regardless of their experience, background, role, or level of contribution.

## 2. Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect, patience, and professionalism.
- Communicate constructively and remain open to different perspectives.
- Provide useful, honest, and respectful feedback.
- Accept criticism of ideas and work without turning disagreements into personal attacks.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and consent.
- Give appropriate credit for other people's work and contributions.
- Focus discussions on relevant technical, creative, or community matters.
- Help maintain an inclusive and welcoming environment.

We value thoughtful disagreement. Differences in technical opinions are welcome when discussed respectfully.

## 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or degrading comments based on personal characteristics or identity.
- Threats, stalking, or deliberate intimidation.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith behaviour, or targeted abuse.
- Spam, malicious impersonation, or deceptive attempts to harm the community.
- Deliberately sabotaging projects, contributors, or community activities.
- Retaliating against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.

This list is not exhaustive. Conduct that creates an unsafe, hostile, or persistently disruptive environment may also be addressed.

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within:

- STRATUM-managed GitHub organizations and repositories.
- STRATUM-managed Discord servers and other official community spaces.
- Project discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, and collaboration channels.
- Events and activities conducted in the name of STRATUM.
- Other situations where an individual is substantially representing STRATUM or its community.

The Code may also apply to conduct outside these spaces when it directly affects the safety, well-being, or participation of STRATUM community members.

## 5. Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the STRATUM maintainers.

**Contact email:** stratum027@gmail.com

Suggested subject:

`[CODE OF CONDUCT] Report of Community Concern`

Please include, where possible:

- A description of what happened.
- The relevant date, time, and community space.
- Links, screenshots, or other relevant evidence.
- The people or accounts involved, if known.
- Any additional context that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Please do not submit sensitive reports through public GitHub issues, pull requests, or community channels.

## 6. Handling Reports

Reports will be reviewed by the appropriate STRATUM maintainers.

Maintainers will aim to:

- Review reports fairly and objectively.
- Consider the available evidence and relevant context.
- Respect the privacy of everyone involved.
- Avoid unnecessary disclosure of report details.
- Take appropriate action based on the seriousness and circumstances of the behaviour.

Not every report will result in the same outcome. Decisions will consider the nature, frequency, severity, and impact of the conduct.

We cannot guarantee a particular investigation or response timeline.

## 7. Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, possible actions include:

1. **Reminder:** A private or public reminder of the community standards.
2. **Warning:** A formal notice that the behaviour must stop.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporary limits on participation or access.
4. **Suspension:** Temporary removal from relevant STRATUM spaces or activities.
5. **Removal:** Permanent removal from a community space or project.
6. **Organizational action:** Additional measures concerning organizational access, roles, or collaboration.

Severe misconduct may result in immediate action without prior warning.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the situation and applied consistently.

## 8. Responsibilities of Maintainers

STRATUM maintainers are responsible for:

- Communicating and applying this Code of Conduct.
- Reviewing reports in good faith.
- Maintaining appropriate boundaries and confidentiality.
- Taking reasonable steps to address harmful behaviour.
- Avoiding retaliation against individuals who raise concerns.
- Explaining enforcement decisions where appropriate and safe to do so.

Maintainers are also expected to follow the standards outlined in this document.

## 9. Appeals

Individuals affected by an enforcement decision may submit an appeal by contacting stratum027@gmail.com.

Appeals should explain why the decision should be reconsidered and provide any relevant additional context.

An appeal may be reviewed by an appropriate maintainer who was not directly responsible for the original decision, where reasonably possible.

An appeal does not guarantee that an enforcement decision will be reversed.

## 10. Final Statement

Participation in STRATUM projects and community spaces is subject to this Code of Conduct and any applicable project-specific rules.

We believe strong communities are built through respect, accountability, collaboration, and shared responsibility.

Thank you for helping make STRATUM a constructive place to build, learn, and create.
